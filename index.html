How it works

Three steps. Five minutes.

  1. 01 Offer accepted

    Your branch flags the buyer in your CRM — or just forwards the acceptance email. Conveya texts the buyer within sixty seconds, in your brand.

  2. 02 Conveya runs the file

    The buyer gets a real quote from your panel, completes ID and AML, uploads source of funds, and signs the engagement — all in one conversation.

  3. 03 Solicitor wakes up instructed

    A complete, validated file is pushed to your panel firm overnight. The branch sees a green tick before opening the next morning.

FAQ

The questions every group asks first.

How long does setup take?

Seven working days for a single brand. We need a logo, a colour, a domain, and the panel firms you'd like quotes from. The longest part is usually getting your IT to whitelist the email sender.

Will it work with our CRM?

Yes — we have native two-way connectors for Reapit, Alto, Jupix and SOS Connect. For anything else, a webhook does the job in an afternoon.

What about data protection?

UK-hosted on AWS London. SOC 2 Type II in progress, ISO 27001 controls in place, and a fully documented DPIA we can share with your DPO.

What does the buyer actually see?

Your brand, end to end. Email comes from your domain (we run DKIM/SPF), the web flow is on a subdomain you control, and the only place "Conveya" appears is the policy footer.

What's the contract like?

The pilot is free with no notice period. After that, monthly rolling for the first three months — then annual if you'd like a discount. No exit fees.
